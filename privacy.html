Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

What this application does

Health Sheet Sync is a private Google Apps Script used by its sole author. With the author's explicit consent, it reads daily step counts and sleep duration from the author's own Google Health account and writes those two values into a Google Sheet owned by the author.

Data accessed

Step counts and sleep duration, via the read-only scopes googlehealth.activity_and_fitness.readonly and googlehealth.sleep.readonly. No write access to health data is requested.

Storage and sharing

Data is written only to the author's own Google Sheet. Nothing is stored on any external server, transmitted to third parties, sold, or used for advertising or analytics. There are no other users.

Retention and removal

Authorization can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately stops all access.
